Shashank Karthik Rajan is currently pursuing the B.S. degree in robotics engineering with a minor in electrical engineering at the University of California, Santa Cruz, CA, USA, with expected graduation in 2027. He joined the power electronics lab of Prof. Nathan Ellis as an undergraduate researcher in Spring 2026, where he is working on a closed-loop hysteretic buck converter for rapid line transients at 100–200 V to support deterministic converter evaluation, and on a high-voltage dc–dc converter enabling small conversion ratios at high voltage. He previously worked as a PCB layout automation intern with the Juniper Networks group at Hewlett Packard Enterprise. His research interests include power electronics, high-voltage systems, hybrid switched-capacitor converters, wide-bandgap devices, and aerospace power systems.
