Abraham Osorio is currently pursuing the B.S. degree in electrical engineering at the University of California, Santa Cruz, CA, USA, with expected graduation in 2027. He recently joined the power electronics lab of Prof. Nathan Ellis as an undergraduate researcher. He previously conducted research in the UCSC Autonomous Systems Lab under Prof. Renwick Curry and Prof. Gabriel Elkaim, where he implemented an attitude and heading reference system (AHRS) algorithm for UAV attitude estimation. His design experience includes printed circuit board design for signal integrity, RF telemetry, and power conversion. His research interests include high-density power electronics, switched-capacitor converters, and aerospace power systems.
